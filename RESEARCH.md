# University Medical School In-House Shadowing Programs

## **Confirmed In-House Shadowing Programs:**

### **Harvard University**
- **BIOME Program** (Biomedical Interdisciplinary Occupations and Medical Exposure)
- Winter break intensive shadowing in Boston area
- Structured discussion and reflection components
- [Program Website](https://www.biomeshadowing.org/)

### **UCLA**
- **PULSE Program** (Pre-medical Undergraduate Learning through Shadowing Experience)
- Year-round shadowing at Ronald Reagan UCLA Medical Center
- Departments: Pediatrics, Internal Medicine, Neurology, Cardiology, Emergency Medicine
- Case study presentations and SRP course credit available
- [Program Website](https://pulseucla.wixsite.com/pulse)

### **Stanford University**
- **SIMS Program** (Stanford Immersion in Medicine Series)
- Quarter-long shadowing at Stanford Hospital, Lucile Packard Children's Hospital, and Palo Alto VA
- Available to sophomores, juniors, and seniors
- [Program Information](https://advising.stanford.edu/beyond-undergrad/planning-medical-school/sims)

### **Duke University**

- [Shadowing Information](https://advising.duke.edu/prehealth/shadowing/)

## **Why Universities Would Want a Centralized Shadowing Platform**

### **Operational Efficiency**
- **Centralized Management**: Instead of managing shadowing programs through emails, spreadsheets, and manual coordination, they could streamline everything in one platform
- **Automated Matching**: Pair students with appropriate physicians/specialties based on interests, availability, and requirements
- **Scheduling Integration**: Coordinate complex hospital schedules with student availability

### **Scale and Access Issues** 
- **Limited Capacity**: Most programs have waitlists or restricted access (like Duke's program for "selected students" or Emory's "limited number" of observerships)
- **Geographic Constraints**: Rural schools like Cornell struggle with hospital access - your platform could connect them to virtual or remote opportunities
- **Resource Allocation**: Schools like Princeton with no affiliated medical school could access shadowing networks they can't provide internally

### **Quality Control & Tracking**
- **Hour Documentation**: Automated tracking of the 40-150+ hours different schools require
- **Learning Outcomes**: Track what students actually learn vs. just time spent
- **Physician Feedback**: Structured evaluation system for both students and mentoring physicians

### **Student Experience Enhancement**
- **Specialty Exploration**: UCLA PULSE offers 5 departments - your platform could offer exposure to dozens
- **Personalized Learning**: Unlike Harvard BIOME's fixed winter program, provide year-round flexible scheduling
- **Portfolio Building**: Integrate with application systems for medical school admissions

### **Data & Analytics**
- **Program Effectiveness**: Universities could measure which shadowing experiences correlate with medical school acceptance
- **Alumni Networks**: Connect current students with graduated physicians
- **Compliance Reporting**: Automated documentation for accreditation requirements

**The Gap**: Even top-tier programs like Stanford SIMS are limited to their own hospital systems. A centralized platform could provide access to diverse healthcare settings nationwide.
